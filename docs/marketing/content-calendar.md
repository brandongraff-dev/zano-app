# 30-Day Starter Content Calendar — Phase 0

Source: `docs/spec.md` §22 ("Phase 0... start the build-in-public series. Post daily."). Covers the
first 30 days of daily posting across TikTok / IG Reels / YouTube Shorts, before code/TestFlight is
ready, building toward the Phase 1 TestFlight opening at ~week 4 (spec §22).

**Content pillars (spec §22), on rotation:**

| # | Pillar |
|---|---|
| 1 | The shield moment (screen recording of trying to open TikTok → blocked → "gym is 6 min away") |
| 2 | Earned unlock celebration after a workout |
| 3 | Founder progress (own streak, rank, protein) — authentic, imperfect |
| 4 | Weekly Report Card screenshots |
| 5 | "Locked out" reaction clips from friends/testers |
| 6 | Educational: why willpower fails, why the phone is the lever |

Rotation is a straight 1→6 cycle repeated 5 times across 30 days. Pillar 4 (Report Card) lands every
6th day here for simplicity; once real dates are picked, shift those specific posts to whichever day
is the actual Sunday, since the in-app Report Card itself generates Sunday night (spec §5.14) and the
screenshot should be fresh, not staged early.

Caption drafts below are placeholders — swap in any of the 25 hook variations from
`docs/marketing/hooks.md` where it fits better. CTA arc: days 1–21 build the waitlist, days 22–30
count down to the Phase 1 TestFlight opening (spec §22 puts this at week 4) — don't promise a public
App Store download inside this 30-day window, that's Phase 2 (week 6–8).

  evenly and double down on those instead of continuing the fixed 6-pillar cycle.

  matching hook from `docs/marketing/hooks.md` when one lands better for the day's format.
