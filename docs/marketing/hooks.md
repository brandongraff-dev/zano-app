# Hooks — 5 Variations Each

Source: `docs/spec.md` §22, "Hooks to test (make 5 variations each)". Five seed hooks below, each
expanded into 5 variations that change the **angle**, not just the wording:

- **Stat-led** — opens on a number (screen time, workouts, hours reclaimed)
- **POV** — second-person, puts the viewer in the moment
- **Confession** — first-person, admits a flaw or a weird thing you did to fix it
- **Challenge** — dares the viewer to try it
- **Before/After** — contrasts a clear "then" and "now"

Use these as first-line hooks (spoken or on-screen text) over the formats in
`docs/marketing/content-calendar.md`. Per spec §22 Phase 3: once 2–3 hooks are clearly
outperforming, drop the rest and double down on those instead of continuing to rotate all 25 evenly.

---

## Seed 1 — "My phone won't let me open TikTok until I hit the gym."

1. **Stat-led:** "I spent 4.5 hours a day on TikTok. Now I can't open it until I've hit the gym."
2. **POV:** "POV: you try to open TikTok and your phone says 'go to the gym first.'"
3. **Confession:** "I had to make my own phone lock me out of TikTok because I have zero willpower."
4. **Challenge:** "Lock TikTok behind a gym check-in for 7 days. I dare you to try it."
5. **Before/After:** "Before: 3 hours of TikTok before I even got out of bed. After: it's locked until I've trained."

## Seed 2 — "I made my phone earn-only."

1. **Stat-led:** "The average person unlocks their phone 96 times a day. Mine only unlocks the fun stuff after I've earned it."
2. **POV:** "POV: your Instagram is locked until you've hit your protein goal."
3. **Confession:** "I got so bad at moderating my own screen time that I built an app to do it for me."
4. **Challenge:** "30-day challenge: make every distracting app on your phone earn-only. Here's how I did it."
5. **Before/After:** "Before: doom-scrolling in bed for an hour straight. After: I earn every scroll with a workout first."

## Seed 3 — "Day 30 of earning my screen time."

1. **Stat-led:** "Day 30: 41 hours of screen time reclaimed, 22 workouts, 0 days I opened TikTok for free."
2. **POV:** "POV: it's day 30 of your phone refusing to let you slack off."
3. **Confession:** "Day 30 update: I almost quit on day 6. Here's what kept me going."
4. **Challenge:** "Day 30 of earning my screen time — I dare you to try 7."
5. **Before/After:** "Day 1 vs. day 30: same phone, completely different relationship with it."

## Seed 4 — "POV: your phone is your gym buddy now."

1. **Stat-led:** "POV: your phone has dragged you to the gym 22 times this month whether you liked it or not."
2. **POV:** "POV: your phone is your gym buddy now, and it does NOT let you skip leg day."
3. **Confession:** "Not me needing my phone to guilt-trip me into the gym like an actual gym buddy would."
4. **Challenge:** "Let your phone be your gym buddy for one week. Bet you don't skip a single session."
5. **Before/After:** "Before: my gym buddy was a text I left on read. After: my phone won't let me skip."

## Seed 5 — "The lock screen widget that fixed my consistency."

1. **Stat-led:** "This lock screen widget took my workout consistency from 2 days a week to 5."
2. **POV:** "POV: you check your lock screen and it's staring at you like 'you haven't earned TikTok yet.'"
3. **Confession:** "I've tried every habit tracker there is. The only thing that actually worked was a widget that locks my apps."
4. **Challenge:** "Put this widget on your lock screen for a week and try to ignore it. You can't."
5. **Before/After:** "Before: 12 unused habit apps. After: one lock screen widget I actually can't ignore."

---

**Total: 25 variations (5 seeds × 5 angles).** Rotate these into the caption/first-line slot of the
content calendar; swap in whichever angle fits that day's pillar and format.
