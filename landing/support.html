Support

Stuck, or something not working? Email support@zano.app. A real person reads every message, usually within two working days. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

Locks and unlocking

I need my apps right now. How do I get out?
There is always a way out. Open ZANO, go to the Lock tab and hold the emergency unlock button. The block screen also has a button that takes you straight there. Using it never deletes anything; it just ends the current lock.
My apps stayed locked after I finished a goal.
Open ZANO once. Goals checked by Apple Health (workouts, steps, sleep) can arrive a few minutes late, and opening the app makes it check again. If it still doesn't unlock, use the emergency unlock and email us.
How do I stop ZANO from locking anything?
Turn off your lock schedules on the Lock tab. To remove ZANO's access completely, go to iOS Settings, Screen Time, and turn off access for ZANO, or delete the app.

Goals

My gym visit didn't count.
Gym check-ins need Location set to "While Using" or "Always" for ZANO and a short stay inside the gym's area. Check the gym's pin in Settings, Gym, and make sure it sits on the building.
Can I log a goal by hand?
Yes. Every goal has a manual way to log it, so a missing Health permission never leaves you stuck.

Subscription

How do I cancel or change my plan?
Apple handles billing. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before renewal to avoid being charged.
I paid but ZANO still shows the paywall.
In ZANO open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you paid with.
Can I get a refund?
Apple handles refunds: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I delete my data?
In ZANO open Settings and tap Delete all my data. That removes your goals, streaks, lock sets, gyms, tags and history from your iPhone. If you signed in, the same screen deletes your account and what we store for it.
Where can I read how ZANO uses my data?
In our Privacy Policy. The apps you block are never sent off your iPhone.
